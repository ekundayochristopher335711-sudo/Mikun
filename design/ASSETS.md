# MIKUN visual assets

The mark in `../public/mikun-logo.png` was rebuilt from the logo image supplied in the conversation. It preserves the green chef and loop symbol, white MIKUN lettering, and “…GOOD FOOD, GOOD VIBE…” lockup on a transparent background. The source image was visible in chat but was not supplied as a downloadable file, so this is a close reconstruction rather than an original vector master. The high-resolution working image is `logo-reference-reproduction.png`.

Brand green used across the UI: `#26df00`. The transparent logo should never be recoloured or stretched. The landing header provides a small contrast plate behind it so the white lettering remains readable.

Every food and drink image in `../public/photos/` is a real licensed photograph. The source and license pages are listed in `../public/photos/SOURCES.md`. Stock images illustrate the cuisine and are not presented as MIKUN's actual meals or premises.
